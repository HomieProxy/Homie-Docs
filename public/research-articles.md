### Academic Search Engines

* [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com) – Broad, discipline-agnostic search across articles, theses, books and court opinions.
* [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org) – Free, open catalogue indexing 250 M+ scholarly works with strong coverage of OA and Global South literature.
* [BASE](https://www.base-search.net) – Bielefeld University’s engine crawling 240 M+ open-access documents from 8 000+ providers.
* [CORE](https://core.ac.uk) – Aggregates millions of open-access papers and links you straight to full-text PDFs.

### Journal & Publisher Platforms

* [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com) – Elsevier platform for peer-reviewed STM and social-science journals, books and reference works.
* [JSTOR](https://www.jstor.org) – Digitised back-files of journals, books and primary sources, especially strong in humanities & social sciences.
* [SpringerLink](https://link.springer.com) – Journals, books and conference proceedings across STEM and business fields.
* [Wiley Online Library](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com) – Full-text journals and ebooks spanning science, medicine and social sciences.
* [Taylor & Francis Online](https://www.tandfonline.com) – Journals covering arts, humanities, social sciences and technology.

### Preprint & Working-Paper Servers

* [arXiv](https://arxiv.org) – Flagship open preprint archive for physics, math, CS and more (2.4 M+ papers).
* [SSRN](https://www.ssrn.com) – Social-science and economics working papers; 1.6 M+ drafts shared “tomorrow’s research today”.
* [RePEc](https://repec.org) – Decentralised network of economics working papers, articles and software components.
* [bioRxiv](https://www.biorxiv.org) – Life-science preprints.
* [medRxiv](https://www.medrxiv.org) – Clinical-research preprints.

### Theses & Dissertations Repositories

* [OATD](https://oatd.org) – Global index of open-access master’s and doctoral theses.
* [EThOS](https://ethos.bl.uk) – UK doctoral dissertations, digitised on demand.
* [DART-Europe](https://www.dart-europe.org) – Portal to European research theses.
* [NDLTD Global ETD Search](https://search.ndltd.org) – Worldwide electronic theses & dissertations.
* [HAL](https://hal.science) – French national open-access repository for scholarly documents.
